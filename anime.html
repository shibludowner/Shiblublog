🍥My Favorite Anime

Samurai Champloo


📖click to see my review

Personal rating:★★★★★ 5/5

A one-of-a-kind journey that blends Edo-period Japan with hip-hop culture. Directed by Shinichirō Watanabe, Samurai Champloo follows Mugen, Jin, and Fuu as they travel across Japan in search of the mysterious "samurai who smells of sunflowers." With stylish sword fights, unforgettable music by Nujabes and Fat Jon, and characters full of heart, it feels both timeless and effortlessly cool.

Naruto

★★★★★

More than just a story about ninjas, Naruto is a journey of perseverance, friendship, and chasing impossible dreams. Watching Naruto grow from an outcast into a hero makes every victory feel earned and unforgettable.

Bleach

★★★★★

Stylish, emotional, and packed with unforgettable characters, Bleach delivers some of the greatest sword fights and transformations in anime. Its soundtrack, world-building, and the Thousand-Year Blood War arc make it an all-time favorite.

Vinland Saga

★★★★★

A breathtaking tale of revenge, war, and redemption that slowly transforms into a deeply human story about peace, forgiveness, and what it truly means to be strong.

Attack on Titan

★★★★★

A masterpiece of mystery, sacrifice, and freedom. Every revelation changes the story completely, while its unforgettable characters and breathtaking twists make it one of the greatest anime ever created.
